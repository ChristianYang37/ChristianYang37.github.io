## Research {#research-overview}

My research combines theory and experiments to understand how modern machine learning systems learn, reason, and use information. I am interested in LLM inference, fine-tuning, reasoning and agents, the science of deep learning, and trustworthy and data-private machine learning. I especially enjoy connecting mathematical structure to measurable model behavior and working implementations, and I am open to new directions such as world models.

<div class="research-grid">
  <article class="research-card">
    <p class="research-card__index">01</p>
    <h3>Efficient LLMs & Agents</h3>
    <p>Sparse attention, KV-cache compression, speculative decoding, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and systems for reasoning and agents.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="research-card">
    <p class="research-card__index">02</p>
    <h3>Trustworthy & Secure AI</h3>
    <p>Copyright-aware optimization, data privacy and recovery, calibration, and the foundations of safe and reliable model behavior.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="research-card">
    <p class="research-card__index">03</p>
    <h3>Learning Dynamics & Theory</h3>
    <p>Scaling laws, optimization and generalization, representation geometry, and how pretraining and reward learning shape model capabilities.</p>
  </article>
</div>
